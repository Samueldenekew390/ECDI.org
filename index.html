About Organization

ABOUT ECDI

The Ethiopian Community Development Initiative (ECDI) is a community-focused Ethiopian organization committed to sustainable development, professional opportunities, skills development, research, community participation, and social, economic, and environmental progress.

The organization aims to connect qualified Ethiopian graduates with professional opportunities and community-development initiatives. ECDI focuses particularly on graduates from Social Science, Natural Science, and other relevant academic disciplines.

Professional Capacity Scale

ECDI aims to develop opportunities for up to 758 professionals, subject to available positions, organizational capacity, funding, and applicable recruitment requirements across urban and regional programs.

Dedicated to Sustainable Growth

  • ✓ Empowering youth and graduates with hands-on professional experiences.
  • ✓ Conducting grassroots social research and participatory monitoring.
  • ✓ Promoting environmental protection, green development, and climate resilience.

COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AREAS

EDUCATION AND SKILLS DEVELOPMENT
